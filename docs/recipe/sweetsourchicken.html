Sweet & Sour Chicken

Prep: 25 mins | Total: 34 mins

Servings: 4 +

Sweet and sour chicken is a popular dish in Chinese-American cuisine, consisting of crispy fried chicken pieces and vegetables coated in a tangy sweet sauce.

Flavor Breakdown

  • Sauce: consists of sweet; sugar, ketchup, and pineapple juice
  • Sour: Vinegar; apple cider, white distilled, or rice vinegar
  • Other flavors: Saltiness from soy sauce, thickening agent from cornstarch

Ingredients

Main Ingredients: 6 oz pineapple, 2 oz carrot, 3 oz red bell pepper, 2 oz onion, 12 oz chicken breast, 10 oz corn oil

Marinade Ingredients: 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp light soy sauce, 2 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp water, 1 tsp salt

Chicken Preparation

  1. Combine chicken with all the ingredients listed under "Chicken" except for egg, cornstarch, and oil. Let it marinate if you have the time. Right before frying, mix in the egg and cornstarch.
  2. Shallow or deep fry in batches until the chicken pieces are crispy and cooked through. Set aside.

Sauce and Finish

  1. In a saucepan, combine all the ingredients listed under "Sauce" except cornstarch, toasted sesame seeds, and chopped scallion. Let it come to a boil.
  2. Make a slurry by mixing cornstarch with a little bit of water in a cup. Pour the slurry into the sauce and stir well. Adjust seasonings.
  3. Add the chicken pieces to the sauce and simmer for 10 minutes. Cook uncovered if you want it to be drier until you reach the desired consistency.
  4. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds (optional) and chopped scallions.
